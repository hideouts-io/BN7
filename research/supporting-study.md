## Step S1 — Frontier Intelligence Workflows

**Source:** `36366e96c12765e14d09965c1f82330194dfa8d3`; source version `0.9.0`.

**Purpose, users, and problem.** FIW helps a reviewer turn an uncertain technology claim into a record another person can challenge. Its position in BN7 is the public workflow layer: organize collection, assessment, and reassessment while retaining human decision authority. The central problem is that claims, repeated reporting, interpretation, and forecasts can otherwise acquire stronger status as they circulate. The source describes deterministic checks over declared records, rather than independent discovery of truth. This is relevant to researchers, technical diligence teams, analysts, and decision owners evaluating consequential claims. [Role and scope, README lines 5–13](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-intelligence-workflows/blob/36366e96c12765e14d09965c1f82330194dfa8d3/README.md#L5-L13).

**Inputs → workflow → outputs.** Start with a decision question, claims, evidence references, provenance, assumptions, alternatives, and consequences. A reviewer builds the evidence record, distinguishes observation from inference, records contradictions and unknowns, and identifies the next evidence and responsible owner. Outputs include a Decision Record, evidence cards, integrity findings, and a Decision-Ready Brief describing a supportable next move, countercase, decision hinge, stop conditions, and reconsideration evidence. The brief remains advisory. [Workflow and handoff, README lines 15–59](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-intelligence-workflows/blob/36366e96c12765e14d09965c1f82330194dfa8d3/README.md#L15-L59).

**Implementation inspected.** Perception Integrity checks whether claim modes agree, observations overlap with inferences, evidence IDs resolve, and high-stakes records declare multiple primary roots. Source Genealogy groups declared derivatives and checks independence counts, while preserving retracted, superseded, and unavailable evidence. Language Integrity checks declared forecast/result, attribution, inference, and supersession relationships. Frontier Signal Integrity adds frozen prediction criteria, discriminating tests, update receipts, and reassessment while preserving upstream findings. These checks rely on declared relationships; they do not establish whether real sources are independent. [PI code, lines 327–423](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-intelligence-workflows/blob/36366e96c12765e14d09965c1f82330194dfa8d3/scripts/perception_integrity.py#L327-L423), [genealogy code, lines 239–286](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-intelligence-workflows/blob/36366e96c12765e14d09965c1f82330194dfa8d3/scripts/source_genealogy.py#L239-L286), [FSI code, lines 168–250](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-intelligence-workflows/blob/36366e96c12765e14d09965c1f82330194dfa8d3/scripts/frontier_signal_integrity.py#L168-L250).

**Current versus proposed.** The repository contains Python evaluators, JSON contracts, synthetic fixtures, tests, release tooling, templates, and a local HTML teaching experience. The proposed next independent experiment in its diligence example is a hypothetical human choice, not a performed experiment or investment recommendation. A source-defined test checks that five derivative URLs remain one provenance root. `NO_FINDINGS` means no configured rule found a problem in the supplied record; it does not mean the underlying claim was verified. [Example boundary, lines 21–57](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-intelligence-workflows/blob/36366e96c12765e14d09965c1f82330194dfa8d3/examples/frontier-technology-diligence/README.md#L21-L57), [test, lines 73–84](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-intelligence-workflows/blob/36366e96c12765e14d09965c1f82330194dfa8d3/tests/test_source_genealogy.py#L73-L84), [limits, lines 9–35](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-intelligence-workflows/blob/36366e96c12765e14d09965c1f82330194dfa8d3/LIMITATIONS.md#L9-L35).

**Public hook:** “Three reports can still be one source.” Use the fictional diligence case to show how tracing origins changes the next question without declaring the technology false. This is the strongest short-playbook candidate in this group.

## Step S2 — Pax Silica Intelligence

**Source:** `2f62f678d06f8444ddc7788ce03e1f1f46cf4cba`; source version `0.3.3`; dataset review boundary `2026-09-29`.

**Purpose, users, and problem.** This is BN7's public-source intelligence implementation for strategic technology supply chains. It makes sources, claims, participation, technology dependencies, capability, and uncertainty inspectable for readers examining industrial development. Its role differs from FIW's teaching examples: its corpus contains attributed real public publications. Its interpretation remains explicitly labeled and dated; inclusion does not establish qualification, investment merit, or mission readiness. [Role and evidence boundaries, README lines 3–36](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/pax-silica/blob/2f62f678d06f8444ddc7788ce03e1f1f46cf4cba/README.md#L3-L36), [snapshot, data lines 1–22](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/pax-silica/blob/2f62f678d06f8444ddc7788ce03e1f1f46cf4cba/data/pax-silica.json#L1-L22).

**Inputs → workflow → outputs.** Canonical JSON separates source records from claims, events, signatories, and programs. Evidence states distinguish Official, Secondary, Reported Development, Analysis, Unknown, and Superseded. Validation checks dates, source references, review deadlines, and public records; a Python builder generates the static site. Rendered source entries expose publisher, publication/review dates, supported claims, limitations, and an original-source link. JavaScript supports the participant map, supply-chain layers, capability stages, and evidence drawer. [Data envelope, schema lines 1–94](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/pax-silica/blob/2f62f678d06f8444ddc7788ce03e1f1f46cf4cba/data/schemas/pax-silica.schema.json#L1-L94), [source rendering, builder lines 168–204](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/pax-silica/blob/2f62f678d06f8444ddc7788ce03e1f1f46cf4cba/scripts/build_web.py#L168-L204), [interaction source, app lines 7–65](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/pax-silica/blob/2f62f678d06f8444ddc7788ce03e1f1f46cf4cba/web/app.js#L7-L65).

**Current versus proposed.** Site-generating code and reviewed records exist. The README identifies a public website destination, but this study did not inspect its current deployment. The dated corpus includes policy and development reporting; a project's announced future stages are not observed industrial capability. The interactive readiness ladder explains the separate evidence required for Announced, Funded, Building, Operating, Qualified, and Producing. That is an educational ontology, not a measured score or a BN7 certification of any facility.

**Evidence demonstrated and limits.** Static inspection confirms explicit source/claim separation and executable date/reference checks. An integrity script checks that public referenced sources appear in generated HTML. Mutation-style tests define rejection of source or record verification dates later than the corpus snapshot. Their existence does not demonstrate current source accuracy, independent corroboration, live freshness, or user-tested accessibility. Public factual claims borrowed from this corpus require independent current source review before publication in the playbook. [Integrity code, lines 97–143](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/pax-silica/blob/2f62f678d06f8444ddc7788ce03e1f1f46cf4cba/scripts/check_evidence_integrity.py#L97-L143), [snapshot tests, lines 40–87](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/pax-silica/blob/2f62f678d06f8444ddc7788ce03e1f1f46cf4cba/tests/test_snapshot_boundary.py#L40-L87).

**Public hook:** “An announcement is the start of a capability story.” Follow an industrial claim through the evidence needed for funded, operating, qualified, and repeatable output. The drawer's “what this supports / what remains unestablished” approach is a useful playbook design pattern.

## Step S3 — AI Cyber Assurance

**Source:** `9033bf73bbd3923c5de0a9c6fb9959b4c140de64`; source version `0.6.1`; Assurance Case schema `0.3`.

**Purpose, users, and problem.** ACA is BN7's AI/cybersecurity domain implementation. Builders, operators, security reviewers, governance teams, auditors, founders, and system owners use it to connect system scope and authority to threats, controls, evidence, recovery, and a human decision. It addresses fragmented reviews in which documents, evidence, conditions, and corrective actions can drift apart. It provides templates and bounded automation; the source does not establish secure production systems or customer outcomes. [Purpose and role, README lines 3–45](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/ai-cyber-assurance/blob/9033bf73bbd3923c5de0a9c6fb9959b4c140de64/README.md#L3-L45).

**Inputs → workflow → outputs.** Choose a bounded Quick Review or a broader Full Assurance Lifecycle, collect applicable records in an approved working package, record evidence classes and findings, apply the decision rubric, and preserve owners, conditions, and retests. Optional JSON represents exactly one bounded human decision and generates a Decision Receipt, Assurance Passport, and Executive Summary. These are views of the same case, not separate sources of authority. [Case and invariants, Assurance Intelligence lines 27–98](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/ai-cyber-assurance/blob/9033bf73bbd3923c5de0a9c6fb9959b4c140de64/13-assurance-intelligence/README.md#L27-L98).

**Implementation inspected.** The validator checks typed references, required human authority, evidence collection dates, conditions for Amber decisions, and closure prerequisites. A closed finding needs linked corrective action, a passing matching retest, and non-Unknown evidence. The renderer escapes case-controlled Markdown/HTML, validates before generating, and refuses existing output overwrites unless explicitly requested. The JSON schema documents local constraints; executable Python handles cross-object chronology and relationships. [Closure code, lines 367–426](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/ai-cyber-assurance/blob/9033bf73bbd3923c5de0a9c6fb9959b4c140de64/scripts/validate_assurance_case.py#L367-L426), [renderer lines 267–284](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/ai-cyber-assurance/blob/9033bf73bbd3923c5de0a9c6fb9959b4c140de64/scripts/render_assurance_outputs.py#L267-L284).

The fictional supplier-research agent begins with excess write authority, an unbounded credential, and missing logging evidence; its records describe remediation and retest. These are synthetic events. The example does not exercise an actual agent or validate real controls. The release review explicitly separates simulated UAT from observed representative-user studies and assistive-technology testing. [Example lines 7–56](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/ai-cyber-assurance/blob/9033bf73bbd3923c5de0a9c6fb9959b4c140de64/10-examples/synthetic-ai-agent-assurance/README.md#L7-L56), [UAT boundary lines 91–99](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/ai-cyber-assurance/blob/9033bf73bbd3923c5de0a9c6fb9959b4c140de64/RELEASE_REVIEW.md#L91-L99).

## Editorial implications

The strongest shared explanation is a sequence: identify a claim, trace its evidence, keep uncertainty visible, record the next bounded step, and preserve human accountability. FIW teaches the method; Pax Silica presents attributed public-source intelligence; ACA and Quantum apply bounded review in specific domains. This is a useful reader-facing interpretation of the inspected sources, not a demonstrated integrated production pipeline.

For the short playbook, begin with the three-reports/one-root story, then offer one paragraph per domain and links to its authoritative guide. Define “assurance” as a reasoned, evidence-linked basis for a bounded judgment. Keep test definitions, structural validation, fictional outcomes, and real-world effectiveness distinct. Reserve academic expansion for methodology comparison, reproducibility, evidence-class distinctions, source independence, and evaluation design. Treat business adoption, performance improvements, revenue, affiliations, and external validation as facts still requiring supplied evidence.
