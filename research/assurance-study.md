## 1. Bridge-Node-7

**Snapshot:** `3f959138f3252b16bc69dabf178bdde83a606a3b`, cloned at `/Users/macbookpro/Codex/Any/BN7-sources/Bridge-Node-7`.

**Purpose and users.** This repository is the public identity and architecture guide. It describes “Frontier Assurance Infrastructure” for engineering teams, researchers, mission programs, and strategic institutions. Its organizing question is what an accountable person can justify now, and which change would require reconsideration. It contains documentation rather than an executable product. The public playbook should use it to explain the common purpose connecting the implementation repositories. [Positioning and public-reference scope](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/Bridge-Node-7/blob/3f959138f3252b16bc69dabf178bdde83a606a3b/README.md#L3-L32).

**Inputs → workflow → outputs.** A bounded decision question starts evidence acquisition and challenge; relevant domain assurance and dependency analysis prepare a concise human view; a person decides; receipts, reviewed memory, observed outcomes, and reassessment preserve the reasoning. The path is compositional: teams activate only the systems material to their uncertainty. These are documented architectural responsibilities; this repository does not demonstrate their complete integration or operational adoption. [Lifecycle](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/Bridge-Node-7/blob/3f959138f3252b16bc69dabf178bdde83a606a3b/docs/DECISION_LIFECYCLE.md#L9-L54).

**Connections and authority.** The architecture assigns evidence lineage and institutional memory to Intelligence Library, research methods to Frontier Intelligence Workflows, assurance semantics to FMA, dependencies to Mission Graph, and decision preparation to Frontier Decision Engine. The person retains consequential judgment. Names in this authority map do not by themselves establish public implementation, deployments, customers, or system availability. [Authority map](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/Bridge-Node-7/blob/3f959138f3252b16bc69dabf178bdde83a606a3b/docs/FRONTIER_ASSURANCE_ARCHITECTURE.md#L42-L75).

**Public narrative opportunity.** Explain one question at three depths: an opening understandable in a minute; the decision, alternatives, uncertainty, and next evidence; then technical sources. This follows the existing Decision Surface pattern, which is a presentation pattern rather than another product or record system. The proposed short and long playbooks can share one narrative with deeper authoritative links. [Progressive disclosure](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/Bridge-Node-7/blob/3f959138f3252b16bc69dabf178bdde83a606a3b/docs/DECISION_SURFACE.md#L63-L97).

**Limits and publication questions.** “Less hidden uncertainty” is the intended value; no measured reduction in time, risk, or cost is supplied here. Public visibility is not proof of operational use or broad reuse rights. `LICENSE` reserves original-content rights; the user's stated BN7 authorization covers this playbook work, while reader-facing software rights must remain repository-specific. `EXECUTION_AUTHORITY.md` distinguishes repository, release, and production permissions; it is research evidence, not a reason to interrupt the already authorized drafting.

## 2. frontier-mission-assurance

**Snapshot:** `f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230`, cloned at `/Users/macbookpro/Codex/Any/BN7-sources/frontier-mission-assurance`. Source package version: `0.11.2`; Python ≥3.11; runtime dependency PyYAML ≥6.0; `fma` CLI. Source version is not a claim about the latest published release.

**Purpose and users.** FMA helps technical leaders, scientists, engineers, and reviewers reconstruct the support for one consequential decision when assumptions or evidence change. Existing laboratory, notebook, source-control, and supplier records remain authoritative. The visible implementation is a Python package, portable schemas, domain profile validators, synthetic examples, and release controls, with generated Markdown reports rather than a hosted public dashboard.

**Implemented workflow.** YAML/JSON assurance graphs carry missions, claims, assumptions, evidence, dependencies, and decisions. Validation checks versions, node identities, required fields, permitted kinds/statuses/relations, references, and selected semantics. Evidence coverage counts declared direct evidence links on critical nodes; it does not authenticate evidence or establish scientific sufficiency. Dependency impact traverses declared `depends_on`/`requires` relations. Decision verification checks allowed dispositions, rationale, explicit reopen conditions, and basis references. [Graph validation](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance/blob/f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230/src/frontier_assurance/validate.py#L67-L179), [coverage and impact](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance/blob/f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230/src/frontier_assurance/analysis.py#L19-L62), [decision verification](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance/blob/f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230/src/frontier_assurance/decision.py#L20-L75).

Research receipts separately verify declared integrity and acceptance. Fresh reproduction stages declared code and inputs without prior outputs, binds direct entrypoint execution, executes with a timeout, then verifies newly produced artifacts. It is an integrity boundary with host capabilities, not an execution sandbox. [Reproduction implementation](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance/blob/f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230/src/frontier_assurance/receipt.py#L324-L455).

**Flagship short-playbook example.** Use the checked-in synthetic neutral-atom case. Its movement-loss assumption changes from `baseline` to `degraded`. The comparison marks the reused resource estimate stale, identifies evidence outside its declared conditions, reopens expert review, and propagates consequences to the decision. The baseline is already **HOLD**, because a decision-gating applicability basis is declared rather than established. Tell the reader that revised conditions require review; do not imply the software approved, rejected, or experimentally tested a quantum architecture. [Baseline disposition](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance/blob/f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230/profiles/ftqc-assurance/examples/synthetic-neutral-atom/baseline/decision-receipt.yaml#L1-L14), [comparison logic](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance/blob/f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230/scripts/compare_ftqc_cases.py#L171-L275), [corroborating test expectations](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance/blob/f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230/tests/test_ftqc_case_compare.py#L44-L58).

**Other domains and long-version opportunities.** Scientific Discovery Assurance separates proof-checker PASS from specification equivalence, replication, priority, and attribution. Its validator rejects overall PASS with unresolved equivalence or incomplete reproduction. Orbital Recovery Assurance keeps physical state, digital trust, authority, and requalification separate; its reference kernel evaluates declared options and models. Its 5,000-case benchmark is synthetic; protocol history explicitly preserves invalidated approaches. These profiles offer academic teaching examples, not demonstrated research discoveries or satellite recovery outcomes. [Scientific controls](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance/blob/f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230/scripts/validate_scientific_discovery.py#L89-L123), [orbital benchmark scope](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance/blob/f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230/profiles/orbital-recovery-assurance/benchmark/README.md#L7-L36).

**Gaps to resolve before publication.** Public-source code alone establishes neither adoption nor quantified business outcomes. The `0.11.2` release receipt calls the source a candidate awaiting hosted validation and immutable-release protection; verify current release metadata separately before claiming published stable status. The Scientific Discovery profile contract says “Containing FMA release: 0.8.0”; clarify whether this means introduction or current containment.
