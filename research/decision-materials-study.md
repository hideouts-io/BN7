## Study 1: Frontier Decision Engine

**Purpose and readers.** FDE organizes technical, organizational, mission, and strategic decisions so an accountable person can inspect the choices, evidence limits, tradeoffs, next proof, and recorded action. Prospective users include program leaders, engineering reviewers, strategy teams, and advisors. Those roles are inferred use cases, not evidence of existing customers.

**Inputs and workflow.** A browser-local application first accepts ordinary-language context. `draftFromInput()` extracts candidate decisions, choices, criteria, futures, and source-linked decision hinges using bounded textual patterns. The person receives a working brief and may stop there. Optional formal comparison requires a human-selected bounded set of 2–3 strategies, 2–4 objectives, and 2–4 scenarios; the minimum is 2 × 2 × 2. Numerical assumptions remain explicit human or analyst inputs. There is no remote AI inference or external factual retrieval in the documented default runtime. [Architecture, lines 7–43](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-decision-engine/blob/4a913756d00cdd321da04bf7350507e571f2f2dd/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#L7-L43), [extraction implementation, lines 213–240](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-decision-engine/blob/4a913756d00cdd321da04bf7350507e571f2f2dd/site/src/universal-ui.js#L213-L240).

**Implemented calculation.** Each comparison cell uses baseline plus scenario modifier, bounded to 0–100, then compares it with the declared objective threshold. Missing or invalid values remain invalid. The ranking minimizes critical-failure scenarios, then compares worst-case critical pass rate, worst-case pass rate, overall pass rate, and critical-failure count. Ties and insufficient information are explicit outputs. A comparative leader remains separate from assurance posture and human authorization. Scores are desirability assumptions, not measured probabilities or forecasts. [Calculation, lines 235–278](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-decision-engine/blob/4a913756d00cdd321da04bf7350507e571f2f2dd/site/src/lib/decision-core.js#L235-L278), [ranking, lines 352–373](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-decision-engine/blob/4a913756d00cdd321da04bf7350507e571f2f2dd/site/src/lib/decision-core.js#L352-L373), [method limits, lines 17–21](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-decision-engine/blob/4a913756d00cdd321da04bf7350507e571f2f2dd/docs/METHODOLOGY.md#L17-L21).

**Outputs and authority.** Outputs include a working Decision Brief, formal comparison, portable Decision Case, reassessment delta, and Decision Receipt v2. Receipt construction requires accountable-owner/delegated-decider authority and explicit human attestation. Separate SHA-256 digests bind substantive content and the receipt envelope. Residual uncertainty must retain unresolved evidence and its rationale. This is an attestation with integrity checks, not verified identity, a digital signature, organizational approval, or proof of decision correctness. The local receipt archive does not prove complete chronological continuity. [Receipt implementation, lines 102–160](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-decision-engine/blob/4a913756d00cdd321da04bf7350507e571f2f2dd/site/src/lib/recording.js#L102-L160), [receipt boundaries, lines 19–37](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-decision-engine/blob/4a913756d00cdd321da04bf7350507e571f2f2dd/docs/DECISION_RECEIPTS.md#L19-L37).

**Relationships and limits.** FDE accepts Mission Graph Decision Context Packet 0.3.0 only for preparation. Integrity, origin, evidence assurance, freshness, and human authority remain distinct. A self-asserted authenticated origin is rejected; non-current context cannot enter active preparation. The handoff is memory-only. PRIVATE/PROTECTED are BN7 handling labels with explicit non-release semantics; their presence does not turn the public deployment into an approved confidential environment. Direct M2M case import into FDE was not established by this study. [Context contract, lines 37–65](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-decision-engine/blob/4a913756d00cdd321da04bf7350507e571f2f2dd/schemas/mission-graph-decision-context-0.3.0.schema.json#L37-L65), [trust checks, lines 176–200](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-decision-engine/blob/4a913756d00cdd321da04bf7350507e571f2f2dd/site/src/lib/governed-context.js#L176-L200).

**Proof posture.** Checked-in project facts report 226 tests and seven browser modes; tests examine missing data, human authority, residual uncertainty, and receipt tampering. They were read, not rerun. Pilot documentation defines usefulness/reuse/reconstruction gates; it is a pilot design, not a completed user study. Browser-local storage is not encrypted confidential storage. Apache-2.0 is the inspected repository license.

The archived synthetic qualification fixture mixes materials terminology with cameras, synchronized viewpoints, signatures, and known-target controls. Avoid reproducing it unchanged in the short playbook. The current built-in case in `decision-core.js`, lines 5–68, has a clearer synthetic sourcing narrative: current source, second-source qualification, and temporary reserve.

## Study 2: Materials-to-Mission

**Purpose and readers.** M2M makes a material or component dependency traceable from evidence to a defined human decision. Intended readers include materials researchers, engineering teams, manufacturing/supply-chain reviewers, and program decision owners. It addresses scattered evidence and hidden uncertainty, without becoming a supplier-qualification authority.

**Inputs and workflow.** A canonical case contains a Decision Charter, complete Material Assurance Record, compact Decision Passport, and provenance. Evidence records require source, locator, dates, basis, claim state, applicability, requirement links, limitations, classification, AI involvement, and human reviewer. Python performs structural validation first, then the explicitly selected semantic profile. [Case schema, lines 6–16](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/materials-to-mission/blob/2a8d26af86e8adb8b1e34550045782d1a20418a4/schemas/case.schema.json#L6-L16), [evidence schema, lines 6–22](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/materials-to-mission/blob/2a8d26af86e8adb8b1e34550045782d1a20418a4/schemas/evidence-record.schema.json#L6-L22), [profile dispatch, lines 668–696](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/materials-to-mission/blob/2a8d26af86e8adb8b1e34550045782d1a20418a4/src/materials_to_mission/validator.py#L668-L696).

**Implemented safeguards and outputs.** Triggered critical conditions block `ADVANCE` and `PARTNER`; they must remain visible by exact identifier in the Passport. Unknown, partially supported, contradicted, unsupported, and expired evidence cannot disappear into favorable summaries. Validation returns actionable findings and exit codes; rendering validates before generating Markdown. The Passport presents the owner, evidence posture, governing weak link, alternatives, recommendation, next action, acceptance criteria, proof requirements, stop rule, and reassessment trigger. [Critical-condition rule, lines 250–281](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/materials-to-mission/blob/2a8d26af86e8adb8b1e34550045782d1a20418a4/src/materials_to_mission/validator.py#L250-L281), [render gate, lines 160–175](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/materials-to-mission/blob/2a8d26af86e8adb8b1e34550045782d1a20418a4/src/materials_to_mission/cli.py#L160-L175).

**Public examples.** The fictional Alloy M-17 case deliberately contains missing origin evidence and conflicting capacity statements. Its declared human disposition is HOLD, with a request for independently reviewable origin/lot evidence. GA-001 is a frozen reviewed public-source Gallium snapshot dated August 10, 2026. YIG-001 is reviewed public research context for an engineered material system. The 60-mineral Atlas is a navigation field, not 60 completed reviewed pathways. BN7 Application Lenses are a presentation ontology, not a government risk/readiness ranking. Public-source evidence is real source material; the synthetic case remains fictional, and neither establishes BN7 operational outcomes. [GA-001 scope, lines 8–22](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/materials-to-mission/blob/2a8d26af86e8adb8b1e34550045782d1a20418a4/public-snapshots/gallium/GA-001/README.md#L8-L22), [Atlas limits, lines 12–14](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/materials-to-mission/blob/2a8d26af86e8adb8b1e34550045782d1a20418a4/public-snapshots/materials-field/MF-001/README.md#L12-L14).

**Interoperability.** A deterministic exporter emits FMA Assurance Graph v1, Decision Receipt v1, and a projection manifest with source/output hashes. It retains richer M2M evidence fields in extensions and explicitly declares the projection lossy. The source M2M case stays authoritative. Pinned-schema conformance is distinct from compatibility with current FMA implementations. No Research Receipt is manufactured without an actual experiment. [Interoperability, lines 15–64](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/materials-to-mission/blob/2a8d26af86e8adb8b1e34550045782d1a20418a4/docs/FMA_INTEROPERABILITY.md#L15-L64).

**Maturity and limits.** M2M explicitly remains M0 experimental public method. Checked-in validation reports passing synthetic checks and at least 95% combined coverage; this study did not independently rerun them. Human mobile, assistive-technology, and first-time comprehension UAT remain unattested. The browser is a derived, read-only view; Python semantic validation remains authoritative. MIT is the inspected license, with NOTICE retained. [Maturity, lines 5–23](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/materials-to-mission/blob/2a8d26af86e8adb8b1e34550045782d1a20418a4/docs/MATURITY_AND_PROOF.md#L5-L23).

## Recommended short-playbook demonstration and remaining work

Lead with the fictional actuator pathway: a promising bench result cannot fill an origin-evidence gap. Show the missing link, human HOLD, next proof, and compact Passport. Then offer FDE as the optional decision-framing/comparison layer. Describe that connection conceptually until an exact-version integration is demonstrated. This gives public readers a concrete story, academics inspectable methodology, and partners/investors a bounded evaluation route.

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory site
```

The five-minute M2M evaluation script explicitly uses older compatible profile `m0-strict-0.2.0`; disclose that profile if using its output. Before publication, execute the current-profile demonstration, preserve outputs/hashes, verify source-versus-live identities, record exact-version ecosystem compatibility, and test novice comprehension. Verify time-sensitive Gallium policy claims at their primary sources before reuse. No commercial adoption, qualification, mission success, or investor-return evidence was established.
