## Working brief

Choose one audience and a feasible first conversation so business development can prepare a focused introduction and target list. This is the first practical step toward the conversation kit; the proposed audience and problem require confirmation.

**Plain-language introduction.** BridgeNode7 develops methods and reference software that help teams trace the evidence behind important technical decisions. Its work connects claims, assumptions, supporting records, and human review so teams can examine what needs reconsideration when an input changes. This wording follows the [public playbook](PUBLIC_PLAYBOOK.md#how-the-work-connects).

**Audience hypothesis.** Begin with technical leads at consultancies that review AI or cybersecurity systems. Investigate their own review workflow first. Whether a suitable organization would become a buyer, referral partner, or delivery partner remains undecided. This segment is proposed because BN7 already documents an [AI/cyber partner workflow](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/ai-cyber-assurance/blob/9033bf73bbd3923c5de0a9c6fb9959b4c140de64/13-assurance-intelligence/partner-kit/START_HERE.md); the guide does not establish market demand.

**Problem hypothesis.** After a system, its evidence, or a controlling assumption changes, reviewers may spend effort reconstructing which earlier conclusions still apply and what needs another review. We need a recent example from a practitioner to establish whether this is a meaningful problem in the chosen segment.

**Proposed first conversation.** Hold a 20-minute discovery discussion about one decision, its owner, the current review process, and what changed. Use a non-confidential overview, consistent with the [published partner route](https://bridgenode7.com/partner/). Success is a documented problem, an explicit lack of fit, or a clear next question.

**Possible evaluation to discuss.** If there is a fit, a BN7 technical owner could scope an evaluation of that decision. The [documented FMA approach](https://github.com/Bridge-Node-7/frontier-mission-assurance/blob/f99dd2174c74f26b648321e02f8d30e2cff10230/docs/USE_AND_EVALUATION.md#governed-one-decision-evaluation) offers a basis for identifying relevant evidence, unresolved assumptions, and conditions for renewed review. Delivery capacity, outputs, timing, commercial terms, and operational-use rights remain unconfirmed; this draft makes no service commitment.

**Materials to use.** Use the existing playbook and its explicitly synthetic changed-assumption example for preparation. The [review brief](REVIEW_BRIEF.md) identifies the current PDF/HTML and review status. Public examples explain the method; customer results and time savings remain unestablished in the [evidence register](research/EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md#c20--business-evidence-remains-unestablished).

**CEO input needed.** Confirm audience and relationship role; the available engagement and delivery owner; and usable materials, business evidence, and priority relationships. The three questions below capture these decisions.

## First prospect conversation

This is a proposed discovery format, not a technical assessment. Record ordinary professional context and keep the initial overview non-confidential.

| Minutes | Purpose | Usable prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Introduce your role and the reason for the conversation. | “I work in business development with BridgeNode7. We are exploring how teams keep the evidence behind technical decisions useful as systems change. I would like to understand your current process.” |
| 3–11 | Understand one actual decision and its owner. | “Could you describe a recent review or decision, who owned it, and how the supporting evidence was organized?” |
| 11–16 | Investigate changed conditions and present effort. | “When the system or an important assumption changed, how did you decide which earlier conclusions needed another look? Which part took the most effort?” |
| 16–20 | Establish whether another step would be useful. | “What output would make that review more useful, and would a discussion with our technical owner help clarify a possible fit?” |

Capture the decision, accountable role, reason for acting now, changed condition, current evidence process, difficulty described, desired output, fit or lack of fit, and next action with its owner. A practitioner confirming a problem is evidence of that person's situation; it does not establish broad market demand.
